# Tunga vs Thieves — every situation (DRAFT 2026-11-17, for the owner to check)

The rules (RULES_ABSOLUTE.md) say what is ALLOWED. This file says what can HAPPEN between people at every moment:
who learns what, every pairing of teams, and every way each person can respond. Each row becomes (1) a screen state,
(2) a bot behaviour, (3) something the balance check measures.

**V** = villager · **T** = thief · **S** = a Stone · "silent" is always a choice for everyone, everywhere.

---

## 0. Talk — allowed any time, never checked for truth

| Talk move | Examples |
|---|---|
| Claim own role | "I am Kisaan" (true or false) |
| Claim own team | "I'm with the village" |
| Accuse | "Raju is a thief" · "Raju is Chor" (exact role) |
| Vouch | "Raju is clean" · "Raju is with me" |
| Stone claim | "Asha has a Stone" · "I have a Stone" · "I have no Stone" · "The Stone went to Raju" |
| Report a card | "My Talashi: Raju holds Faisla + Kundli" · "My Kundli: Raju is Daku" (true, half-true or invented) |
| Deny | "That's a lie" · "I never had it" |
| Ask | "Who has the Stone?" · "Raju, what did you see?" |
| Plead | "Don't shoot me, I'm Kisaan" · "Don't vote me" |
| Agree / back up | "+1 to Asha" |
| React | 😭 cry · 😁 cheer · 😱 shock · 🙄 not buying it · 😤 angry · 🤫 shh |

**Who talks when:** everyone, any phase — **out players too** (owner 2026-11-17): an out player who knows something (a Kundli result, where a Stone went) can keep helping — or misleading — the table.
**Thieves do NOT know each other** (owner 2026-11-17). Every thief starts alone; thieves find each other only through Kundli, reports, or behaviour — so a thief can hurt another thief by mistake, and a "secret handshake" in talk is a real tactic.

| Who | Learns | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| Each player | own role + team · the list of ALL roles in play (so the team counts) | silent · claim a role at once (true/false) · claim team · a claim of a role not in play is a provable lie · two claims of the same role = one liar |
| V | — | early vouching for others is a guess |
| T | only their own role — no idea who the other thieves are | lie about role from turn 1, or stay quiet · hint to find fellow thieves ("handshake" talk) |

## 2. Picking up the pile (every turn)

| Picker finds | Picker | The passer (knows what they passed) | Others |
|---|---|---|---|
| a Stone, picker V | keep it (hide it) · pass it on · announce "I got a Stone" · cheer | if passer V: relieved/vouch "I passed it to a friend" · if passer T: tracks it, may accuse the picker later | ask "who has it?" · accuse the picker on a hunch |
| a Stone, picker T | keep it quietly · fake-announce "nothing here" · pass it to a fellow thief | if passer V: may realise they handed it to a thief → cry/accuse | — |
| both Stones | same, doubled — a V may keep both (wins if held to the end) | — | — |
| Dal Badal, picker T | keep for later · use now | — | — |
| Dal Badal, picker V | pass it on (useless to them) · keep it to deny thieves · announce "I hold Dal Badal" (true/false) | — | — |
| a pair | play now · hold for a better moment (timing) | — | — |
| nothing useful | pass · bluff "I found something" | — | — |

## 6. Kundli — the player and target see each other's role (only those two)

| Pair | What happens | Player | Target | Others |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V sees V | both now trust each other | silent · vouch publicly · keep it secret (a hidden ally) | silent · vouch back · (V target may doubt and stay quiet) | a V-V vouch is strong; thieves may fake-vouch to copy it |
| V sees T | V knows a thief; T knows V knows | accuse now · stay silent and wait for Faisla / final vote · use Teer Kaman later (sure hit) | counter-accuse first ("she's the thief!") · silent · fake-vouch "she's clean" (to confuse) · use Dal Badal to change role (makes the accusation wrong!) | must pick a side: word against word |

| Dal Badal later | a known role can change — an old Kundli report can become false without anyone lying | — | — | — |

## 7. Faisla — everyone votes; most votes is out

**Timing choices for the caller:** early (little info) · after a Kundli/Talashi report · right after a Stone moved · late (just before the final vote) · to save a friend (no: a Faisla can't save, only remove).

| Caller | Wants out | Case | Caller's play | Target | Others |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V | a T (sure, from Kundli) | strong | accuse + vote | plead · counter-accuse · Dal Badal (B: any time) | follow or not |
| V | a suspected T | weak | accuse + vote · or not vote and let the table decide | plead | split |
| T | a V (holding a Stone) | — | accuse falsely + vote | plead · report truth | split |
| T | a T (Chor eliminates a thief) | to look innocent · or that T is exposed anyway (cut losses) | vote for the T | plead / reveal / accuse the caller back | — |
| any | nobody in particular | fishing | play it, then abstain | — | — |
| caller votes | for anyone · not at all | — | — | — | — |
| during the vote | accusations, vouches, pleas, reactions any time | — | — | — | — |
| outcome | out = V / out = T / tie (nobody) | caller gets the cards (maybe a Stone!) · out player gives the extra vote (to a friend, or to frame someone) | — | — | reactions: cheer, cry, shock |
| caller voted out | own Faisla backfires | their 3 cards go to the next player as the pile | — | — | — |

## Bot memory and hunches (owner 2026-11-17)
- Memory is imperfect like people's: details fade turn by turn, big moments (robbed of a Stone, accused, a Teer Kaman,
  a Faisla) fade slowly, impressions stay; some bots are sharp, some forgetful; rarely two players get mixed up.
- Hunches come from memory of players' ACTIONS (votes, Faisla timing, Teer Kaman targets, Stone thefts, silence when
  accused, contradictions over time), not only from talk.
- Nobody blabbers: a hunch is said once when it forms — and it RESURFACES at big moments (a Faisla, the final vote, a
  Teer Kaman aim), where people bring up old suspicions again.
- Base: the existing bot brain (deduce.ts exact deduction, fading memory, 3 skill levels, docs/BOT_LOGIC.md) moved
  onto the new rules.
