## The world and the look (decided by the designer)
- **The online game must look like the printed box** — it sells the board game. Use the designer's art, never invent.
- **Card game only**: dark playmat; seats = the printed **card back** (dimmed, lit only on that player's turn);
  your own role card face up; the pile = face-down cards. No shrine/basket props on the table.
- **Cover** (`art-source/cover.png`) = home screen. **Empty shrine scene** (`art-source/shrine-empty.png`) = final vote
  + game over (Stones land on the steps if the village wins).
- **Fonts**: keep the app's own fonts (designer: titles needn't match the card lettering).
- **Hand card** = art + title only (title drawn in code over the frame); tap any card = full rules.
- **Known role (Kundli / put out)**: shown as the full card face up on your phone (designer chose option B).
  OPEN QUESTION the designer raised: "only for his turn" — ask which: (1) face up only while it's that player's turn,
  or (2) face up only during the turn you learned it, then back (still in ☰ → My secrets).
- **Nudges are GREEN** (`.nudge`, `.nudge-dot`, `.nudge-ring` in globals.css): the next step glows, new info glows.
  Never on lethal/irreversible moves (Teer Kaman Shoot, Dal Badal), no fake urgency (CCPA 2023 dark-pattern rules).
  **No text tips** — removed; the thing to tap glows instead.
- `DESIGN.md` ("Lantern Haveli", hooded figures) and `docs/ART_BRIEF.md` are **SUPERSEDED** by the above.
