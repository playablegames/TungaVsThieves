# Tunga vs Thieves — absolute rules for the app (ALL GAPS ANSWERED 2026-11-17; Dal Badal timing + elimination flow await the balance check)

The app cannot "adjust" the way a table does, so every step, edge case and tie is written down here.

## 2. A turn

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Pick up the 3-card pile. You hold 5. | [P] |
| 2.2 | Then EITHER play a matching pair of one action card, OR play nothing. Only one pair per turn. | [P] |
| 2.3 | Only the 6 action cards can be played as pairs. **Stones have no power** — they are only held or passed. **Dal Badal is played as a single card** (it is the only single card with a power). | owner Q5 |
| 2.4 | Every turn is ONE choice: **PLAY or PASS**. PLAY a pair: you hold 3 → pass all 3 → draw 2 from the draw pile at the end of your turn (back to 2). PASS: keep any 2, pass the other 3. | owner Q6 |
| 2.5 | **Playing nothing**: keep any 2, pass the other 3 face down to the next player. | [P] |
| 2.6 | You pass **any 3 cards, face down** — Stones and Dal Badal included. Nobody sees the pile until the next player picks it up. | owner Q7 |
| 2.7 | **Draw pile runs out**: shuffle all the discards into a new draw pile. If even that is not enough, the player draws what is left and holds fewer than 2 until cards come back. | owner Q8 |
| 2.8 | **Turn timer: 20 seconds.** If time runs out the app PASSES for you: keeps 2 at random, passes the other 3, never plays a pair. | owner Q9 (20 s); timeout = pass as proposed |
| 2.9 | A round ends when **every living player has had one turn**, counting from the first player. Out players are skipped, so rounds get shorter. | owner Q10 |

## 3. Action cards (each needs a matching pair)

| # | Card | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Talashi | Pick any living player; only you see their 2 action cards. | [P] |
| 3.2 | Hera Pheri | **STEAL, not swap** (owner 2026-11-17: the printed "draw 2 & swap" text is wrong). You take both cards of any living player. Flow: pair → you hold 3 → steal their 2 → you hold 5 → pass 3 → hold 2, no draw. The robbed player **draws 2 straight away**. ⚠ How to play page 6 still says "Draw 2 cards, then swap 2" — fix. | owner Q11 + Q11b |
| 3.3 | Kundli | Pick any living player; you and they see each other's role, in secret. | [P] |
| 3.4 | Faisla | **Every living player votes, the caller too. No voting for yourself.** The player with the **most votes is out — plain most votes, no majority needed.** **You may choose not to vote (a "No vote" button).** 20 s to vote; no vote in time = no vote. **OPEN VOTE (owner 2026-11-20): every vote shows to everyone the moment it is cast, and any player may CHANGE their vote until the vote closes — so the table can agree on one target. The vote closes when the 20 s run out, or earlier: once everyone has voted, 5 s after the last change.** | owner Q12 + 2026-11-20 |
| 3.5 | Faisla tie | **A tie for most votes = no result, nobody is out.** | owner Q12 |
| 3.6 | Bhukamp | Every living player passes **1 card — any card, Stones and Dal Badal included —** to the next player, all at once, in secret. The player who played it joins in with the 3 they hold, then passes 3 and draws 2 as normal. 20 s; time out = a random card is passed. | owner Q13 (timer as proposed) |
| 3.7 | Teer Kaman | Pick a living player and name a role — **the exact role** (Chor ≠ Daku). **Name 1 role at 4–6 players, 2 roles at 7–12** (a hit if either is right). The app checks and announces **HIT** or **MISS** to everyone; the role itself stays secret. Hit: they are out. Miss: you are out. | owner Q14 |

## 4. Dal Badal

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Only a thief can use it, on their turn. The thief shows their role to everyone and picks 3 other living players. Those players swap role cards and play on with the new roles. | [P] |
| 4.2 | **The thief's own role card is in the shuffle: thief + 3 = 4 role cards**, shuffled and dealt back face down, one each. The thief may become a villager; a villager may become a thief. Each sees only their new card. | owner Q15 |
| 4.3 | **Dal Badal is that turn's play** — no pair the same turn. A thief holding it chooses PLAY A PAIR / PLAY DAL BADAL / PASS. After Dal Badal they hold 4: pass 3, keep 1, draw 1 at the end of the turn. | owner Q5 + Q16 |
| 4.4 | Fewer than 3 others alive: **the thief picks all of them** (e.g. 2 others left → 3 role cards shuffled). | owner |
| 4.5 | A villager holding it can never use it; it is passed like any card. | [P] |

### ✅ Dal Badal timing — DECIDED (owner 2026-11-17)
**Only on the thief's own turn**, as that turn's play (4.1, 4.3) — "just do Dal Badal on their turn, simple". Never out
of turn, never during a vote or a Teer Kaman. Team sizes per 1.2 (owner 2026-11-17: 5 = 4+1, 7 = 5+2, 8 = 6+2, 10 = 6+4).

## 5. Elimination

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Only Faisla or Teer Kaman can put a player out. | [P] |
| 5.2 | Out by Faisla: your cards go to the player who played Faisla. | [P] |
| 5.3 | Right Teer Kaman: the shooter takes the target's cards. Wrong: the shooter is out, and their cards go to the next player. | [P] |
| 5.4 | Nobody ever holds too many: **Faisla (someone else out)**: caller holds 3 + their 2 = 5 → pass 3 → hold 2, no draw. **Teer Kaman hit**: shooter 3 + target's 2 = 5 → pass 3 → hold 2, no draw. **Teer Kaman miss**: the shooter is out; their 3 cards go to the next player AS THE PILE. **Faisla caller voted out by their own Faisla**: same — their 3 cards go to the next player as the pile. (Balance-subject.) | owner Q17 |
| 5.5 | The out player must give an extra vote to a living player. It is used once. A newer gift (from a later out player) cancels the older one. | [P] + [C] |
| 5.6 | The extra vote can be used in **a Faisla vote OR the final vote — the holder chooses (a tick box: "Use my extra vote here", their vote counts 2). Used once, then it is gone for good.** | owner Q18 |
| 5.7 | **Instant win**: if every thief is out the village wins at once; if every villager is out the thieves win at once (no final vote). Sides are counted AFTER any Dal Badal shuffle. | owner |
| 5.9 | **Roles stay secret even after going out.** Teer Kaman: the target shows their role card to the SHOOTER ONLY (hit or miss), then the result HIT/MISS is announced — never the role. Faisla: the out player's role is not shown. An out thief keeps their secret and can keep poisoning the game to the end. | owner 2026-11-17 |
| 5.8 | **Out players CAN**: talk and react freely, any time (claims, accusations, reports, lies) · give their extra vote once, as they go out. **Out players CANNOT**: hold cards, take turns, play or pass cards, vote, use Dal Badal, receive the extra vote, or be picked by any card. The pile always skips them. | owner Q19 + 2026-11-17 |

## 6. The end

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | After the last round, everyone living votes one player out. Tie: nothing happens. | [P] |
| 6.2 | The voted-out player's cards go to the other team: **a thief voted out with a Stone → that Stone counts for the village; a villager voted out with a Stone → it counts for the thieves.** No single player receives them; the game is counted straight after. | owner Q20 |
| 6.3 | Then the Stones are counted. Village wins if villagers hold both; thieves win if any thief holds one. | [P] |
| 6.4 | **A Stone in the 3-card pile when the game ends counts for the VILLAGE** (owner: it makes the village's job easier — the village is the side that needs help). Out players never hold cards (5.8), so no Stone is ever with an out player. | owner Q21 |
| 6.5 | Roles are revealed only at the end. | [P] |
